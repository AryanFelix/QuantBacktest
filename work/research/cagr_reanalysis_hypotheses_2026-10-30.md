# CAGR reanalysis: research-backed hypotheses for the ₹10 lakh / 20% drawdown mandate

**Purpose.** Identify controlled next experiments for lifting net CAGR after the current ETF-mix leaders plateaued at modest returns. This is a research note, not a recommendation or a claim that any candidate will meet the drawdown limit. No backtests were run for this note.

## Findings that change the search

- **Broaden the source of return before increasing leverage.** Published momentum research reports medium-horizon continuation, and India-specific studies have found cross-sectional momentum in historical Indian equities. Those samples are old and do not establish current net returns, but they justify a fresh, point-in-time stock-universe test. [Jegadeesh & Titman (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x), [Dhankar et al. (India momentum study)](https://doi.org/10.1108/jamr-11-2015-0081), [Agarwalla, Jacob & Varma (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0256090917733848).
- **Use official factor indexes as reproducible baselines, not proof.** Nifty200 Momentum 30 ranks six- and twelve-month returns adjusted for volatility; Nifty200 Quality 30 uses ROE, debt/equity and five-year EPS variability; Nifty500 Multicap Momentum Quality 50 combines those momentum and quality inputs. These are useful, rules-based specifications to replicate and compare. The methodology includes periodic rebalancing and buffer rules that reduce turnover. Any pre-inception index history should be separated from live history and treated as backfilled. [Nifty200 Momentum 30](https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty200-momentum-30), [Nifty200 Quality 30](https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty200-quality-30), [Nifty500 Multicap Momentum Quality 50](https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty500-multicap-momentum-quality50), [Nifty equity-index methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/Methodology/Method_NIFTY_Equity_Indices.pdf).
- **Quality is a plausible stabilizer, not a guaranteed CAGR booster.** Test quality as a filter or tie-breaker alongside momentum, rather than claiming its combination will improve returns. India index construction provides a documented model; published results still need replication with current, investable data and costs.
- **Volatility targeting is a risk-control overlay worth testing.** Moreira & Muir report improved risk-adjusted returns when reducing factor exposure after volatility rises, across several markets/factors. This does not establish an India-specific gain or guarantee a 20% maximum drawdown. Compare the same strategy with and without lagged volatility scaling; include the possibility that scaling lowers CAGR or creates turnover. [Moreira & Muir, *Volatility Managed Portfolios*](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w22208/w22208.pdf).
- **Treat trend-following evidence as adjacent, not directly transferable.** Time-series momentum research found persistence over roughly 1–12 months in a diversified liquid-futures sample. An absolute-trend gate on Indian equity exposure is a reasonable separate challenger, but the original evidence is not an India cash-equity result. [Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012)](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/mosk.pdf).
- **Do not use derivatives or intraday frequency as the default route to higher CAGR.** SEBI reported 93% of individual equity F&O traders lost money over FY22–FY24, with aggregate net losses above ₹1.8 lakh crore; its FY23 cash-intraday study found roughly seven in ten individual traders lost. These population studies do not rule out a robust systematic edge, but they make leverage, turnover and realistic transaction costs essential hurdles. [SEBI F&O study release](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/sep-2024/updated-sebi-study-reveals-93-of-individual-traders-incurred-losses-in-equity-fando-between-fy22-and-fy24-aggregate-losses-exceed-1-8-lakh-crores-over-three-years_86906.html), [SEBI cash intraday study release](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/jul-2024/sebi-study-finds-that-7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses_84948.html).

## Highest-priority controlled experiments

1. **India cross-sectional momentum baseline:** monthly rebalance of a *point-in-time* Nifty 200 or Nifty 500 eligible universe; rank by volatility-adjusted 6/12-month returns, with a 1-month skip variant; hold a diversified top basket. Compare a faithful Nifty factor-index replication with a simple equal-weight implementation. Do not assume today’s constituents existed in the historical universe.
2. **Momentum plus quality:** use the official Nifty500 Multicap Momentum Quality 50 rules as a transparent baseline, then test one modest variant that combines the momentum score with ROE, leverage and earnings-stability filters. Record turnover and sector concentration so apparent CAGR is not a hidden concentration bet.
3. **Risk overlays:** on the best frozen equity candidate, compare (a) no overlay, (b) lagged realized-volatility scaling with exposure caps, and (c) one lagged broad-index trend gate. Evaluate each against the ₹10 lakh path and the 20% peak-to-trough ceiling. Treat drawdown as a constraint and report breaches, not merely the average drawdown.
4. **Sector momentum:** test sector-relative ranking only after the stock baseline; use historical index membership and sector classifications. A sector rotation rule can compound concentration and regime risk, so it should be independently evaluated before combination.

## India implementation and research controls

- **Point-in-time data is the gate.** Use historical constituents/membership, delisted stocks, corporate actions, and dividend-inclusive total returns where available. A current-constituent-only stock test has survivorship bias. If those inputs cannot be reconstructed from the available Upstox data, label the result exploratory and do not rank it alongside point-in-time results. Upstox historical candle documentation describes interval and retrieval limits; candle availability alone does not provide historical index membership or a survivorship-free universe. [Upstox historical candle API](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-historical-candle-data/).
- **Use an implementable net-cost model.** Deduct entry and exit brokerage, STT, exchange transaction charges, GST and stamp duty as applicable for the instrument/date, plus a slippage sensitivity. Recompute costs by turnover and trade value; do not assume a single round-trip percentage for cash, ETF, futures and options. [NSE STT computation](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-securities-transaction-tax); user-specific brokerage should come from the actual broker tariff/contract notes.
- **Guard against selection and backfill.** Pre-register a small candidate set, choose parameters using only the training window, and report chronological walk-forward/untouched holdout results. Separate live index history from simulated/backfilled index history. Include a post-selection forward paper-trading period before calling any result a leader.
- **Optimize for the actual mandate.** Rank candidates by net geometric growth subject to the 20% drawdown ceiling, with ₹10 lakh sizing, cash yields set to zero unless tied to an investable instrument, and explicit breach counts. A historical maximum drawdown below 20% is not a guarantee of staying under 20% in future markets.

## Recommendation and limits

The strongest next hypothesis is a diversified, long-only, point-in-time Indian stock momentum portfolio, with official momentum-quality rules as a benchmark and lagged volatility sizing as a risk overlay. It offers a new return source beyond the tested static Nifty/Gold mix while preserving an auditable way to enforce the drawdown objective. The cited papers and index designs justify testing, not selection: old samples, factor crowding, small/mid-cap liquidity, taxes, data survivorship, backfilled index histories and turnover can erase the apparent edge. No source supports promising a particular CAGR or satisfying the 20% drawdown constraint without fresh, correctly costed out-of-sample results.
