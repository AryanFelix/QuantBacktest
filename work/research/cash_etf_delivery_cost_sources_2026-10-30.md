# Indian cash ETF delivery execution costs: primary-source check

Accessed 30 October 2026. Scope: normal, fully funded NSE secondary-market ETF delivery trades through standard Upstox pricing; not intraday, MTF, AMC creation/redemption, or BSE. Rates below are a current-cost scenario, not a historical tariff reconstruction.

| Component | Rate and application | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upstox brokerage | Published standard equity-delivery rate: ₹20 per executed order, each buy and sell. The page says brokerage remains subject to the SEBI prescribed limit. Confirm the account's applicable plan and small-order cap before production use. | [Upstox tariff](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/) |
| NSE transaction fee | From 1 March 2026: ₹306.99 per crore traded value **each side** = 0.0030699%. | [NSE/FA/73061, 27 February 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA73061.pdf) |
| NSE IPFT | From 1 March 2026: ₹0.01 per crore **each side** = 0.0000001%. Transaction fee plus IPFT totals ₹307/crore = 0.00307%; do not add the old IPFT charge to this combined rate. | [NSE/FA/73061](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA73061.pdf) |
| STT: equity-oriented ETF delivery | Purchase: nil. Sale: 0.001% of taxable transaction value, seller pays. Generic stock delivery STT of 0.1% on both legs is inappropriate for these fund units. This ETF rate is unchanged by the 1 April 2026 revision. | [NSE official levy schedule](https://www.nseindia.com/static/invest/first-time-investor-sebi-turnover-fees-stt-other-levies), [NSE/FATAX/73524, 31 March 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FATAX73524.pdf) |

Do not substitute the 0.005% fresh mutual-fund unit issuance levy. | [NSE official levy schedule](https://www.nseindia.com/static/invest/first-time-investor-sebi-turnover-fees-stt-other-levies) |
| SEBI turnover fee | Non-debt securities: ₹10/crore = 0.0001%, buy and sell. NSE separately lists debt securities at ₹2.5/crore; verify instrument classification rather than equating a debt ETF unit with its underlying bond. | [NSE official levy schedule](https://www.nseindia.com/static/invest/first-time-investor-sebi-turnover-fees-stt-other-levies) |
| DP debit charge | ₹20 per scrip per day on delivery sales, plus GST; count once per sold scrip/day, not per partial fill or order. | [Upstox tariff](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/) |

Apply to fees, not trade notional. | [Upstox tariff](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/), [Upstox calculator explanation](https://upstox.com/calculator/brokerage-calculator/), [SEBI circular 2022/0097](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jul-2022/1658208863370.pdf) |

Implementation caveats:

- **Classify STT by actual ETF tax status.** The statutory schedule explicitly refers to equity-oriented fund units. Confirm each non-equity ETF's scheme documentation/current exchange STT treatment rather than applying a uniform equity-ETF or stock rate.
- **Use dated sections and exchange circulars.** Upstox retains older tariff fragments on the same page. Its current table rounds the combined NSE charge to 0.00307%; the circular supplies the exact transaction/IPFT split. Its calculator explanation also retains stale approximate exchange/derivative rates, so use it here only to corroborate the GST base.
- **Historical replay needs effective dates.** NSE's earlier [27 September 2024 circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA64232.pdf) sets cash transaction fees at ₹2.97/lakh each side from 1 October 2024. Current brokerage and DP pricing must not be asserted as historical facts without dated evidence.
- Order counts, same-day scrip grouping, minimum/cap rules and contract-note rounding can change realized rupee totals. Reconcile against actual account contract notes before calling the fee calculation exact. Account maintenance fees and investor income/capital-gains taxes require separate assumptions.
- The shared fee helper now includes 18% GST on the SEBI turnover fee, effective 18 July 2022 when a trade date is supplied and using the current schedule for undated runs. The passive-control sensitivity and stateful 50/200-bps bootstraps were regenerated after the correction. Other strategy outputs have not all been rebuilt; their stored fee totals may omit this small component and need an order-ledger impact check before exact fee totals are cited.
- No actual spread, market impact or slippage was measured. These schedules establish explicit fees only; daily OHLC/close data cannot establish executable bid/ask prices. Use separately labelled execution-cost scenarios unless contemporaneous quote/fill evidence is available.
