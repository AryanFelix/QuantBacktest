# LIQUIDBEES daily NAV source review

**Checked:** 2026-10-27
**Question:** Can official AMFI or Nippon India data supply a daily LIQUIDBEES return history suitable for cash accruals in the backtests?

## Verified from first-party sources

- AMFI's NAV History page offers a text-format query with fund, type, start date, and end date inputs. It states that one download may cover at most 90 days. The visible page does not specify the underlying request parameter names or an API contract. [AMFI NAV Download / History](https://www.amfiindia.com/net-asset-value/nav-download)
- AMFI's public latest-NAV text file identifies the ETF as scheme code `140086`, ISIN `INF732E01037`, name `Nippon India ETF Nifty 1D Rate Liquid BeES`. It separately lists Nippon's newer Growth ETF as scheme `153725`, ISIN `INF204KC1FU1`; that is a different scheme and cannot extend the legacy ETF history. [AMFI latest NAV text](https://www.amfiindia.com/spages/NAVAll.txt)
- Nippon's product page links both **NAV History** and **Dividends**. It describes LIQUIDBEES as a daily-IDCW scheme with compulsory reinvestment; the scheme's stated objective is to track the Nifty 1D Rate Index before expenses, with no guarantee. Its performance disclosure says reported performance uses IDCW-reinvestment NAVs and is based on gross IDCW per unit for this ETF. [Nippon product page](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/FundsAndPerformance/Pages/NipponIndia-ETF-Nifty-1D-Rate-Liquid-BeES.aspx), [scheme information document](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/InvestorServices/SIDETF/NipponIndia-ETF-Nifty-1D-Rate-Liquid-BeES.pdf)
- The fund's product page identifies July 8, 2003 as the ETF inception date. The separate Growth ETF page identifies a July 23, 2025 inception, so its Growth NAV is only a short recent series. [Growth ETF page](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/FundsAndPerformance/Pages/NipponIndia-Nifty-1D-Rate-Liquid-ETF-Growth.aspx)
- The issuer's product page JavaScript calls the public web-service route `https://api.nipponindiaim.com/v1.0/inveasy/services/digitals/FetchDividendDetails` with `Scheme`, `plan`, `Fromate`, and `Todate` query parameters (`Fromate` is the observed spelling). A successful `Scheme=LB`, blank-plan, 2019-06-09 through 2026-07-31 request returned 2,012 daily-IDCW records for plan `DD`, with fields including effective date and retail/corporate dividend per unit. This is an observed issuer web endpoint, not a documented developer API contract.

## What is not yet verified

- The AMFI page is public and renders without a sign-in prompt, but its historical download endpoint was not used for a full NAV history in this review. Its 90-day limit is documented; the specific historical payload format and 2003-present coverage remain unverified.
- The Nippon dividend endpoint returned records only through 2026-07-31 when queried on 2026-10-27, despite the product page showing later NAV/performance data. There are 104 extra rows from repeated effective dates, all in 2019-2020; dates from 2021 onward are unique. Do not assume the older repeated-date rows are separate payments without issuer reconciliation.
- Reinvesting each record as `units *= 1 + DividendRetail / 1000` is a candidate total-return reconstruction for the ₹1,000 face-value scheme, not a validated investor return. For data through 2026-07-31, it grows ₹10,000 to ₹10,430.27 over 1 year, ₹11,432.71 over 3 years, and ₹12,395.37 over 5 years. The issuer's contemporaneous product-page snapshot showed ₹10,451, ₹11,587, and ₹12,426 for those horizons: differences of -₹20.73, -₹154.29, and -₹30.63. The 3-year miss is material, and the service's two-month staleness prevents an aligned check against the product page's newer 2026-09-30 figures.
- NSE's Nifty 1D Rate daily history has 248, 241, 246, 249, 249, and 200 observations for 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 through October 23, respectively, with no weekday-sized gaps in those years. The retrieved 2020 gap from January 28 through August 12 remains. This supports use as a gross benchmark sensitivity on covered dates, not as an investable ETF return.

## Backtest implications

The flat LIQUIDBEES exchange-price candles are not a total-return series. The issuer's dividend records and official reinvestment-basis performance provide a promising reconstruction path, but the feed is stale and the computed return does not reconcile over every horizon. The official Nifty 1D Rate benchmark is continuous from 2021 onward, but has the known 2020 retrieval gap and does not include fund expenses or trading costs.

Until the issuer dividend series is refreshed and fully reconciled, keep the backtests' existing 0% cash-yield treatment; do not promote the dividend reconstruction or gross index benchmark into realized cash returns. Do not fill the 2020 Nifty 1D Rate gap with assumed or synthetic returns. The remaining source task is an AMFI historical-NAV pull in consecutive windows of at most 90 days, filtered to scheme `140086` / ISIN `INF732E01037`, followed by a reconciliation against Nippon's dividend and reinvestment records. The 2025-launched Growth ETF remains useful only for a short recent cross-check.
