# NIFTY intraday option execution challenger — source note

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27  
**Purpose:** identify a new, falsifiable derivative execution test; no performance claim or backtest is made in this note.

## What is already covered

The current research has tested the NIFTY 30-minute opening-range breakout using NIFTYBEES executions, its symmetric short side, opening-range-width filters, and a prior-close SMA200 gate. It has separately screened 30-minute opening-gap fades and daily-marked monthly directional credit spreads. Those ETF intraday rule families lost money net of modeled costs, and the options sample is limited to roughly two years of daily contract candles. The new question is whether a frozen underlying breakout signal, executed as a long option with capped premium risk and minute-level option prices, has a different net payoff from the ETF proxy. Relevant existing records: [ORB baseline method](../../outputs/intraday_orb_range_method.md), [symmetric short-side method](../../outputs/intraday_orb_short_side_method.md), [gap-fade method](../../outputs/intraday_gap_fade_method.md), and [monthly credit-spread method](monthly_nifty_credit_spread_method.md).

## Primary-source data and execution facts

- Upstox's [expired option contracts endpoint](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-expired-option-contracts/) returns contract keys, strike, option type, expiry, lot size, tick size, and weekly status for a specified underlying and expiry. The docs mark this endpoint as Upstox Plus-only (`UDAPI1149`).
- Upstox's [expired historical candle endpoint](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-expired-historical-candle-data/) supports 1-, 3-, 5-, 15-, and 30-minute plus daily bars and returns OHLC, volume, and open interest. It also requires Upstox Plus. The documentation demonstrates a request spanning 2020–2025 for one expired contract but does **not** state a guaranteed earliest date or full-history completeness; probe coverage and empty/partial sessions before freezing any P&L study.
- Upstox's [expired-expiries endpoint](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-expiries/) lists up to six months of historical expiries and is also Plus-only. For older periods, build the expiry calendar from official exchange records, query each explicit expiry, and preserve every missing/empty result rather than assuming the calendar is complete.
- Upstox [regular V3 historical candles](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/) have minute/hour history from January 2022. A request is limited to one month for 1–15-minute bars and one quarter for longer intraday intervals. The existing 30-minute NIFTY signal cache covers 2022-01-03 through 2026-10-23; an index 1-minute series would need separate retrieval if the stop or signal is to be evaluated below 30-minute resolution.
- NSE's [equity-derivatives contract specifications](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-specifications) describe NIFTY weekly/monthly expiries and Tuesday expiry conventions, with the previous trading session used when Tuesday is a holiday. The [market-timings page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings) gives the equity-derivatives session as 09:15–15:40 IST. Use the contract metadata and date-specific calendar rather than hard-coding today's lot size or expiry weekday across the full sample.
- Upstox's [brokerage and statutory charge schedule](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/) lists ₹20 brokerage per executed option order, exchange charges on premium, GST and buy-side stamp duty; option sell-side STT changed from 0.1% to 0.15% on 2026-04-01. Model rates by trade date from the repo's fee audit, and model each leg/order separately. The page says there is no margin requirement for option buying; premium paid is still fully at risk.

## Falsifiable hypothesis to preregister

**Question:** does executing the existing long-only NIFTY 30-minute ORB signal with a one-lot, same-day ATM call produce positive net expectancy at realistic option costs, where its NIFTYBEES execution proxy loses?

Freeze the existing ORB rule exactly: first 30-minute NIFTY candle sets the range; a close above its high on the second candle triggers a long entry at the next available minute's option open. At the signal timestamp, select the nearest listed NIFTY call strike to spot for the nearest expiry not earlier than that session, using the contract response's lot size and tick. Buy at most one lot; close at the last complete 1-minute candle ending before the derivative session close; no overnight position, averaging, short option, or fitted filter. A signal that cannot be joined to a listed contract or fresh positive-volume entry/exit candle is recorded as skipped, not forward-filled. Starting account capital is ₹10 lakh; premium paid plus entry fees is the initial maximum trade loss, and no trade may use more than 0.5% of current NAV. Keep the existing ETF rule as a matched signal/control.

Use 25 bps per side as the primary adverse option-slippage assumption and report 50/100 bps stresses; these are explicit stress inputs, not observed bid/ask spreads. Apply date-specific statutory charges to each buy/sell order, one-lot whole-quantity rounding, and current contract lot-size/tick metadata. Run the full common-coverage set and fixed chronological segments (2022–2023 train, 2024 validation, 2025–2026 pseudo-holdout) from separate ₹10 lakh starts, plus one continuous account. Do not tune strike, entry signal, exit time, cost, or risk after reading the segments.

Reject the hypothesis if net expectancy is non-positive at 25 bps, any independent segment breaches the requested 20% close or conservative intraday drawdown screen, or valid option-bar coverage materially excludes signal days. A pass would only justify prospective paper observation: all historical windows and the ORB family were already inspected, and OHLC bars do not reveal quote spreads, queue priority, or executable fills.

## Gate before any backtest

First make a credential-safe preflight against a small, date-stratified sample of weekly NIFTY expired contracts: verify endpoint entitlement, available expiry coverage, 1-minute candle counts versus NSE sessions, timestamp convention, duplicate/missing bars, positive volume at intended fills, and whether volume/OI units are consistent. Stop and report the gap if the older periods or execution bars are unavailable. The expired API's Plus gate and undocumented historical coverage are the main go/no-go constraints; this note does not claim they have been cleared for one-minute options.

## Preflight outcome — 2026-10-27

The Upstox expired-contract request returned zero contracts for the sampled 2022-09-29 NIFTY expiry. It returned 230 contracts (115 calls) for 2024-10-31 and 288 (144 calls) for 2026-09-29. One nearest-ATM call per sample date returned 375 one-minute bars with positive volume throughout in 2024, and 385 bars with 351 positive-volume bars in 2026. The 2024 sample's last bar was 15:29 IST; the 2026 sample's last bar was 15:39 IST, consistent with NSE's 15:40 equity-derivatives close. These isolated results do not establish continuous expiry, strike, or signal-time coverage. The 2022 sample fails the frozen training-period coverage gate, so no backtest or performance claim was made. Credential-free counts and selected contract keys are in [`nifty_intraday_option_coverage_probe.csv`](../../outputs/nifty_intraday_option_coverage_probe.csv); the raw minute candles were not retained in the repository.
