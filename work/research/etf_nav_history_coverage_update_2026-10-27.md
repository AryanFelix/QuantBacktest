# NIFTYBEES / GOLDBEES NAV coverage update (2026-10-27)

## Finding

**A complete, verified daily NAV total-return series for 2014-01-01 through 2026-10-23 is not established yet.** The official 2016 merger notice resolves the direction: R*Shares Nifty ETF and R*Shares Gold ETF were transferor schemes merged into Goldman Sachs Nifty BeES and Gold BeES, respectively; the Goldman schemes survived and were renamed R*Shares BeES, then Nippon BeES. The surviving-series IDs 503/495 match the Goldman NAVs in AMFI's 2016 daily archive, but Nippon's tested service returns these exact-ISIN series only from 2015-11-01. The lower-valued old-ID responses have no ISIN and are not established as the surviving Goldman schemes, so they must not be spliced into that history. The AMC says transferor-holder allotments used both schemes' NAVs from the last business day before the effective date; the detailed illustration was not located. That illustration is only needed if the old transferor tracks are to be incorporated; it is not required to extend the surviving Goldman NAV series. ([AMC merger notice](https://investeasy.nipponindiaim.com/rmfGsamnew/Documents/NoticetoUnitholdersforTransferofGoldmanSachsMutualFundSchemestoRelianceMutualFund.pdf), [AMFI SAI, Oct 2025](https://portal.amfiindia.com/spages/sai21.pdf), [Nippon NAV history page](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/investor-services/navs), [AMFI Goldman NAV archive, 20-Jul-2016](https://portal.amfiindia.com/NavHistoryReport_Rpt_Po.aspx?frmdate=20-Jul-2016&rpt=0))

## Retrieval and actual coverage evidence

- **AMFI current historical download:** AMFI states that one historical NAV request can cover at most 90 days. A direct `mf=21` query for 2017-01-01–2017-03-31 returned 62 rows for each current ISIN, from 2017-01-02 through 2017-03-31; the 2017-01-02 NIFTYBEES and GOLDBEES NAVs were ₹834.6975 and ₹2,564.7661. A 2026-10-01–2026-10-23 query returned 15 rows for each ISIN. These are successful point-window pulls, not a completed end-to-end extract. A 2014-01-01–2014-03-31 query to the same current Nippon selector returned HTTP 200 but no rows for either exact current ISIN, so this selector did not supply that 2014 window; this result alone does not establish which legacy selector or scheme identifier contains it. ([AMFI history download and 90-day limit](https://www.amfiindia.com/net-asset-value/nav-download); [2017 AMFI query](https://portal.amfiindia.com/DownloadNAVHistoryReport_Po.aspx?mf=21&frmdt=01-Jan-2017&todt=31-Mar-2017); [2026 AMFI query](https://portal.amfiindia.com/DownloadNAVHistoryReport_Po.aspx?mf=21&frmdt=01-Oct-2026&todt=23-Oct-2026); [2014 AMFI query](https://portal.amfiindia.com/DownloadNAVHistoryReport_Po.aspx?mf=21&frmdt=01-Jan-2014&todt=31-Mar-2014))
- **Nippon's first-party NAV-history service:** The AMC NAV-history page calls `MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVs` for historical ranges. On 2026-10-27, requesting its catalogue IDs `503` (NIFTYBEES, AMFI code 140084) and `495` (GOLDBEES, AMFI code 140088) for 2014-01-01–2026-10-23 returned 2,772 rows per scheme, with serialized NAV dates covering 2015-11-01–2026-10-23 after applying the response's `+0530` offset. Separate 2014 requests for IDs 503 and 495 returned no records. Thus this interface did **not** supply the target's full 2014 start in the request tested. ([Nippon NAV history UI](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/investor-services/navs); [NIFTYBEES service query](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/_vti_bin/RMF.Services/MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVs?SchemeIDs=503&NAVPeriod=Historical&FromDate=2014-01-01&ToDate=2026-10-23); [GOLDBEES service query](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/_vti_bin/RMF.Services/MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVs?SchemeIDs=495&NAVPeriod=Historical&FromDate=2014-01-01&ToDate=2026-10-23))
- **2016 merger and old-ID ambiguity:** The AMC's merger notice identifies R*Shares Gold ETF and R*Shares Nifty ETF as transferors into the corresponding Goldman Sachs schemes. Goldman NAVs on the last business day before the effective date determine new units for transferor holders; existing Goldman-scheme holders retain their units. The current Nippon IDs `503`/`495` return ₹872.7431/₹2,914.9140 on 2016-07-20, exactly the Goldman values in AMFI's archive; the old Nippon service IDs `21`/`24` return ₹88.8753/₹2,819.5385 and do not carry an ISIN. Those lower-ID values are consistent with separate unit tracks, but their exact mapping to the transferor schemes is not established by the service metadata. They are **not** verified prehistory for the surviving Goldman scheme. ([AMC merger notice](https://investeasy.nipponindiaim.com/rmfGsamnew/Documents/NoticetoUnitholdersforTransferofGoldmanSachsMutualFundSchemestoRelianceMutualFund.pdf); [Nippon NAV scheme catalogue](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/_vti_bin/RMF.Services/MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVsScheme?CategoryID=0); [current-ID NIFTY query](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/_vti_bin/RMF.Services/MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVs?SchemeIDs=503&NAVPeriod=Historical&FromDate=2016-07-20&ToDate=2016-07-20); [current-ID Gold query](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/_vti_bin/RMF.Services/MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVs?SchemeIDs=495&NAVPeriod=Historical&FromDate=2016-07-20&ToDate=2016-07-20); [AMFI Goldman NAV archive, 20-Jul-2016](https://portal.amfiindia.com/NavHistoryReport_Rpt_Po.aspx?frmdate=20-Jul-2016&rpt=0); [old-ID query](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/_vti_bin/RMF.Services/MobileAppService.svc/GetTrackNAVs?SchemeIDs=21%2C24&NAVPeriod=Historical&FromDate=2016-07-20&ToDate=2016-07-20))
- **2014-era AMFI archive:** AMFI's legacy page is indexed as a per-date “all NAVs for all schemes” report, and its July 2016 page lists Goldman Sachs Nifty and Gold ETFs by their GS scheme names. A direct live fetch of legacy `NavHistoryReport_Rpt_Po.aspx` returned HTTP 500/oversize errors during this audit, so the indexed daily archive was not batch-extracted or reconciled here. The current 90-day interface is usable, but its current AMC selector did not return the two target ISINs for a 2014 window. ([AMFI legacy 2014 daily archive sample](https://portal.amfiindia.com/NavHistoryReport_Rpt_Po.aspx?frmdate=07-May-2014&rpt=0); [AMFI legacy July 2016 archive sample](https://portal.amfiindia.com/NavHistoryReport_Rpt_Po.aspx?frmdate=20-Jul-2016&rpt=0); [AMFI NAV download](https://www.amfiindia.com/net-asset-value/nav-download))

## Investor-return events verified from issuer sources

- **NIFTYBEES:** Nippon's corporate-action table lists record-date payouts of ₹7.00 per unit on 2014-03-11 and ₹8.00 per unit on 2015-02-20, plus a 1:10 split dated 2019-12-20. Its July 2021 factsheet independently confirms the two payouts with cum/ex-dividend NAVs, the target ISIN `INF204KB14I2`, and that face value was ₹10 through 2019-12-19 and ₹1 from 2019-12-20. The AMC table shows no later distribution rows; that is the issuer's published list, not a separate certification that the archive is complete. ([NIFTYBEES corporate actions](https://investeasy.nipponindiaim.com/rmfGsamnew/Products/NiftyBeES/CorporateAction.aspx); [NIFTYBEES July 2021 factsheet](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/investorservices/factsheetsdocuments/fundamentals-july-2021/Innerpage/ETF-Nifty-BeES.html))
- **GOLDBEES:** Nippon's corporate-action table lists a 1:100 split dated 2019-12-20 and no cash-distribution row. Since the page does not explicitly certify a complete no-distribution record, **zero cash distributions remains unverified** on the evidence reviewed. ([GOLDBEES corporate actions](https://investeasy.nipponindiaim.com/rmfGsamnew/Products/GoldBeES/CorporateAction.aspx); [GOLDBEES factsheet identifying ISIN `INF204KB17I5`](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/investorservices/factsheetsdocuments/fundamentals-july-2021/Innerpage/ETF-Gold-BeES.html))
- **Performance convention:** Nippon says scheme performance uses IDCW-reinvestment NAVs / assumes IDCWs are reinvested. That is a performance convention; it does not itself establish that the daily NAV download is already distribution-adjusted. ([Nippon NIFTYBEES performance page](https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/FundsAndPerformance/Pages/NipponIndia-ETF-Nifty-50-BeES.aspx))

## Blockers and next reproducible step

The AMC JSON service returned successfully through a direct first-party HTTP request, but the browser research tool could not open that service URL. AMFI's modern 90-day download worked; its older all-scheme page could not be fetched live in this run. No API key or credential was used. To close the main coverage gap, extract AMFI date-window records for the surviving Goldman schemes back through 2014 and reconcile their IDs/ISINs against AMC and AMFI archive rows, then normalize NAVs to the ETF share basis using verified split factors and payout history. If incorporating the separate R*Shares transferor series, first locate the 2016 exit-option letter and verify its allotment illustration. Until the full date set and event treatment are reconciled, do not use either apparent NAV chain as a complete investor total-return series.
