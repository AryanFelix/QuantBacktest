## Source and artifact notes

- The public [TejHQ dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tejhq/indian-markets) describes its raw NSE tree as exchange-reported daily bhavcopy data and says the `isin` field is the value reported for that date. The local observations above are from `work/data/public_research/tejhq/nse/year=2021/nse_2021.parquet` and `work/data/public_research/tejhq/nse/year=2024/nse_2024.parquet` (dataset revision `2d6629d656fcb715a03b859f7cff8fbd53a7f420`).
- The local TejHQ VBL corporate-action row for 2024-09-12 carries `isin=INE200M01013`, while the issuer’s annual report says the 2024 split changed `INE200M01021` to `INE200M01039`. Treat that action-row ISIN as discordant metadata; use the issuer notice and date-wise raw history for this identity bridge. TejHQ itself cautions that action-feed ISINs may be stale.
- Existing output files and reports contain no IRCTC/VBL rows or these ISINs. In particular, `outputs/corporate_action_entitlements.csv` and `outputs/corporate_action_spos_value_bridge.csv` do not cover these two transitions; this note adds the focused event/identity audit.

### Limitations

The vendor history is a secondary mirror derived from exchange bhavcopies, so its first-observed ISIN date is a vendor observation. This audit did not independently retrieve NSE’s original daily bhavcopy files. It establishes no guarantee that every historical dividend, entitlement, or subsequent identity change is complete.
