# Stock momentum: primary-source return-accounting review

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27  
**Stock bars in current replay:** through 2026-10-23  
**Conclusion:** the NIFTY 50 stock momentum results remain exploratory. The current code handles cash dividends, but does not yet produce a complete position-level total-return ledger for rights, splits/bonus, mergers, or demergers. Do not present the restored-universe result as verified stock P&L.

## What the current replay does

`work/backtest_stock_momentum.py` builds a per-stock signal index from close prices plus parsed cash dividends; the portfolio simulator credits dividends on ex-date to shares held through the prior close. Dividend amounts are converted to Upstox's apparent price-series share units using later split/bonus factors. The conversion remains an inference: the Upstox V3 candle contract describes OHLC, volume, and open interest but does not specify corporate-action adjustment. See the [Upstox Historical Candle V3 contract](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/) and `outputs/stock_dividend_share_unit_alignment.csv`.

The baseline permanently excludes 13 names with action-source records. A restored-universe replay found no selected holding interval crossing a *saved* non-dividend action date, but restored names occupied 84 of 510 top-10 selection slots. This is a useful sensitivity, not proof that action handling is complete: the NSE actions feed is an event-discovery source, not a completeness-guaranteed historical ledger, and the monthly NIFTY membership reconstruction is still provisional. See `outputs/stock_momentum_method.md`, `outputs/stock_momentum_action_aware_method.md`, `outputs/stock_action_exclusion_rank_audit.csv`, and `outputs/nifty50_archive_coverage.md`.

## Specific events that still need an explicit portfolio rule

### Rights issues

The saved NSE corporate-action pull records rights during the test or its 12–1 signal warm-up, including **BHARTIARTL** (2021-09-27, 1:14 at ₹530), **GRASIM** (2024-01-10, 6:179 at ₹1,810), **TATACONSUM** (2024-07-26, 1:26 at ₹817), **UPL** (2024-11-26, 1:8 at ₹358), **ADANIENT** (2025-11-17, 3:25 at ₹1,799), **RELIANCE** (2020-05-13, 1:15 at ₹1,247), and **SHRIRAMFIN** (2020-07-09, 3:26 at ₹560). These are the purpose/ratio/premium labels from the saved NSE feed, not proof that this strategy held each name on the record date. The full source pull is `work/data/universe_sources/nse_corporate_actions_2000_2026.json`.

A rights entitlement is itself a separately credited security. NSE says the eligible shareholder may subscribe, trade/renounce the entitlement, or let it lapse; unsubscribed and unrenounced entitlements lapse. Therefore, a price-only parent series plus ordinary dividends cannot represent a rights event. The backtest needs a frozen policy (for example, sell the RE at a verified exchange price, or subscribe and debit the issue cash), plus the entitlement ratio, record date, RE trading/listing dates and prices, payment schedule, and fees. Do not silently assume subscription or treat a rights issue as a dividend. Sources: [NSE Rights Entitlement FAQs](https://www.nseindia.com/static/trade/members-faqs-rights-entitlement-trading), [NSE Rights Issue process](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/rights-issue), and the [saved NSE corporate-action pull](../work/data/universe_sources/nse_corporate_actions_2000_2026.json).

### Merger and demerger entitlements

The verified entitlement ledger records seven reorganizations. Nine newly issued successors have no cached Upstox daily quote until 23–73 calendar days after the event reference date. A missing row in that cache is an observation gap, not evidence that an entitlement was worthless or that the security could not be traded. Dates, ratios, ISINs, and source filings are recorded in `outputs/corporate_action_entitlements.csv`.

| Event | Entitlement in saved source ledger | First cached successor quote |
|---|---|---:|

| RELIANCE / JIOFIN (2023) | 1 JIOFIN per RELIANCE share | 32 days later |

| VEDL composite demerger (2026) | 1 share in each of four resulting companies per VEDL share | 45 days later for all four |

Use the issuer's scheme and exchange notices to create successor positions on the correct entitlement/record/effective dates. For a daily NAV before a successor has its own quote, define and disclose a defensible valuation policy; do not mark the successor at zero. NSE Indices' demerger rule creates a temporary dummy valued as previous parent close less the ex-date SPOS-discovered parent price, holding that residual until the successor lists. That is an **index valuation convention**, not a tradeable successor price or a general allocation rule. Event-specific SPOS confirmation remains unlocated for ITC and HUL in the local review. For VEDL, NSE's residual is aggregate across four successors. The issuer's cost-apportionment percentages are tax-basis guidance, not market-value weights; they must not be used as fair values. Sources: [NSE equity-index methodology, demerger treatment](https://archives.nseindia.com/content/indices/Method_NIFTY_Equity_Indices.pdf), [NSE SPOS guidance](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-special-pre-open-session), [HDFC Bank merger filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HDFCBANK_19072023144457_SEintimation_Reg34.pdf), [ITC Hotels entitlement filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ITC_27012025211253_SE_COA_.pdf), [Tata Motors demerger filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/TATAMOTORSSJS_12112025224654_NSEBSECOAFINAL.pdf), [HUL entitlement FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HINDUNILVR_29112025134344_SEIntimationFAQs.pdf), and [Vedanta cost-apportionment filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/VEDL_16052026123241_VEDLSEIntimationCostofApportionmentsigned.pdf). The SIEMENS and RELIANCE source URLs are in the event-level ledger.

### Dividends, bonus and split units

The 2020–2026 ever-member-symbol audit contains **646** dividend-labelled rows: 645 parsed amounts and one unresolved VEDL amount (2022-03-09). Ten rows fall in five same-symbol/ex-date groups and need notice-level reconciliation before aggregation. The later-factor conversion applies to 120 parsed rows; 525 rows have no later factor in the saved event feed. The event audit checks date coverage, not source completeness. The 21-event Upstox bonus/split price check found close moves broadly consistent with already-adjusted share units, but that is empirical evidence, not a published Upstox guarantee. Sources: `outputs/stock_dividend_parse_audit.csv`, `outputs/stock_dividend_share_unit_alignment.csv`, `outputs/upstox_split_bonus_adjustment_audit.csv`, and `work/research/nse_corporate_actions_api.md`.

For any split/bonus strategy accounting, choose either raw traded prices with explicit position-unit changes, or consistently normalized prices/volumes plus cash flows converted to those same units. Verify both OHLC fields and quantities across each event; otherwise price adjustment and share-count adjustment can double-count the action. NSE's corporate-actions page exposes purpose, face value, ex-date, and record date, but event terms and revisions still need issuer notices. See the [NSE corporate-actions page](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-actions), [SEBI corporate-actions primer](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/pdf/reference-material/ppt/PT-5%20Corporate%20Action%20-%20Dividends%2C%20Bonus%2C%20Splits%20etc-%20Feb%202025.pdf), and [SEBI split-adjustment circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/mutualfundfile/jul-2018/1530853780625.pdf).

## Official NIFTY 50 total-return benchmark pull

On 2026-10-27, the first-party NSE Indices historical-data page returned daily NIFTY 50 TR and NTR values through its page-backed `getTotalReturnIndexString` route. This route is an observed website interface, not a published public API contract; raw responses are preserved in `work/data/index_daily/nifty50_tri_raw_2020_2026.json`. The normalized history has 1,692 rows from 2020-01-01 through 2026-10-23 and matches the saved NIFTY 50 price-index dates exactly. Reproduction: `work/normalize_nifty50_tri.py`; daily output: `work/data/index_daily/NIFTY50TR-days-1-2020-01-01-2026-10-23.csv`.

For the matched 2021-02-01–2026-10-23 window, ₹10 lakh in the gross official TR series returned **+69.53%**, 9.66% CAGR, and -16.44% maximum drawdown; NTR returned +60.50%, 8.62% CAGR, and -16.64% drawdown. The restored-universe top-10 momentum candidate returned +122.63% / -15.23% drawdown at modeled 10 bps per side and +95.08% / -17.32% at 50 bps per side. In the 2025-01-01–2026-10-23 inspected holdout, the candidate returned +5.74% at 10 bps and +1.84% at 50 bps, versus -2.69% for NIFTY 50 TR. The official index has no direct ETF fees; the NTR series applies publisher tax assumptions, and the stock strategy still has unresolved action accounting. These retrospective comparisons are not evidence of a durable edge or validated stock P&L. Full matched-period rows: `outputs/stock_momentum_benchmark_comparison.csv`.

NSE Indices says TR includes constituent dividends reinvested on ex-date and recommends TR rather than the price index when benchmarking an index-stock investment. The historical data page confirms the publisher-provided series. Sources: [TR methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/resources/index-concepts/total-return-index) and [NSE Indices historical data](https://www.niftyindices.com/reports/historical-data).

## Minimum gate before reporting stock-strategy P&L

1. Freeze an event ledger keyed by instrument/ISIN with issuer source, action type, ex-date, record date, entitlement, issue/payment/listing dates, cash/share changes, and confidence. Reconcile the NSE feed against issuer and exchange notices; preserve revised and same-date notices separately until resolved.
2. Freeze an executable treatment for each action class: dividend cash after any withholding assumption; split/bonus units; rights subscription or sale/lapse of the RE; merger share conversion; demerger successor shares and interim daily marks. Fail closed for unresolved events in a candidate's signal window or holding period.
3. Rebuild the signal total-return index separately from the account ledger. Require event-by-event wealth checks against the action's official terms and quote unit. Keep commissions, statutory fees, and slippage on actual modeled trades; rights subscription cash and any RE sale proceeds are not ordinary trading P&L.
4. Benchmark the selection rule against **NIFTY 50 Total Return**, not only NIFTYBEES/price return: NSE Indices states that a price index omits constituent dividends while TR includes and reinvests them. The official 2020–2026 daily history and matched comparisons are now locally available; use TR as the gross primary benchmark and report NTR separately because its withholding and bonus-share tax assumptions are not automatically the user's personal tax treatment. Sources: [NSE Indices total-return methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/resources/index-concepts/total-return-index), [historical data interface](https://www.niftyindices.com/reports/historical-data), and [NSE Indices NTR launch description](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/2023-12/ind_prs05122023.pdf).
5. Only after those gates, rerun the point-in-time constituent screen with current fees and a pre-frozen walk-forward/forward-paper protocol. The current monthly membership reconstruction covers 2020–2026 but remains provisional; it is not an independent historical universe oracle.

This review is a source and model audit, not a new stock backtest. The 2025+ stock slice has already been inspected, so a corrected replay of it will remain retrospective; fresh paper observations are still required for independent evidence.
