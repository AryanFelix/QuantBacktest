# Primary-source notes for Indian sector ETF rotation

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27  
**Scope:** data, benchmark, fees, and corporate-action constraints for BANKBEES, ITBEES, AUTOBEES, PHARMABEES, and PSUBNKBEES. This memo records source coverage; it makes no performance claim.

## Index and ETF universe

NSE Indices lists Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, Nifty Auto, Nifty Pharma, and Nifty PSU Bank among its sectoral indices. Its [historical-data page](https://www.niftyindices.com/reports/historical-data) has a historical-index selector and a separate “Total returns Index Values” report. The official [TRI explanation](https://www.niftyindices.com/resources/index-concepts/total-return-index) states that a price index excludes constituent dividends, while the total-return index adds and reinvests indexed dividends on ex-dates. Use the sector TRI as a total-return benchmark where available; do not use it as an executable ETF fill price.

Nippon’s product pages identify each ETF’s benchmark and launch date. The common ETF sample is constrained by the youngest fund, AUTOBEES, launched on 20 January 2022; the saved daily cache begins on 25 January 2022. The listed fund histories also start at different times, so extending an ETF-rotation result before the common investable window would introduce synthetic or survivor-biased observations.

## Saved first-party sector index history

On 2026-10-27, the NSE Indices [historical-data report](https://www.niftyindices.com/reports/historical-data) was queried for daily price-index OHLC and gross TRI values for Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, Nifty Auto, Nifty Pharma, and Nifty PSU Bank. The report's current JavaScript maps display names through `IndexMapping.json`, requests `/BackPage/getHistoricaldatatabletoString` for price-index values, and requests `/BackPage/getTotalReturnIndexString` for TR/NTR. Its page code limits each query to 365 days; five chunks per index were used. This is a first-party website interface observed in the page's JavaScript, not a documented public API contract.

Each index returned 1,176 daily values from 25 Jan 2022 to 23 Oct 2026, matching all daily dates in its corresponding Upstox sector ETF market-price history. The TR field is populated; the returned NTR field is `-` for every row, so no sector NTR series is claimed. The two raw captures are `work/data/index_daily/sector_price_index_raw_2022_2026.json` and `work/data/index_daily/sector_tri_raw_2022_2026.json`. `work/normalize_sector_index_history.py` asserts index names, positive values, unique dates, exact date-set parity, and creates `work/data/index_daily/sector_etf_price_pr_tri_panel_2022_2026.csv` plus `outputs/sector_etf_index_total_return_comparison.csv`.

From first common close through 23 Oct 2026, ETF market-price returns were 1.61–7.79 percentage points below the gross sector TRI series. The TR-minus-price-index spread was 5.85–15.50 points. Those are gross benchmark and market-price comparisons, not ETF total-return reconstructions: fund expenses, tracking deviation, market-price premiums/discounts, and cash distributions all affect the ETF-vs-index difference. They do not establish whether distributions were paid by the ETF or on which dates. In 2025-01-01–2026-10-23, each ETF's market-price return was within 0.32–1.13 points of its corresponding gross TRI return. Detailed period metrics are in the comparison output; the sector strategy backtest remains price-only until ETF cash events are reconciled.

The first-party [TR methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/resources/index-concepts/total-return-index) says the index adds constituent dividends on ex-date and reinvests them; [historical data](https://www.niftyindices.com/reports/historical-data) provides separate price-index and TRI reports. Use the TRI for a sector benchmark and keep fills on the traded ETF. Do not relabel TRI index returns as ETF P&L.

## Official AMFI scheme NAV history

AMFI’s [NAV Download page](https://www.amfiindia.com/net-asset-value/nav-download) states that historical NAV downloads are limited to 90 days per request and links its current text report. Its first-party report returned the selected Nippon scheme NAVs by ISIN for 15 consecutive windows from 1 Apr 2023 through 23 Oct 2026. Requests starting before 1 Apr 2023 redirected to the landing page, so this series cannot validate the 2022 sector backtest. Raw selected rows, request URLs, statuses, response sizes, and the exact date mismatches are in `work/data/api_probes/amfi_sector_etf_nav_2023_2026.json`; `work/capture_amfi_sector_etf_nav.py` reproduces the capture and comparison.

The AMFI capture contains 880 NAV dates per ETF. It aligns on 877 dates per ETF with the 882 Upstox/official-index market sessions: five market dates lack AMFI NAV (12 Nov 2023, 2 Mar 2024, 18 May 2024, 1 Nov 2024, and 21 Oct 2025), while three NAV-only dates occur at quarter ends (31 Mar 2024, 31 Mar 2025, and 31 Mar 2026). The comparison keeps only exact shared dates; no values are interpolated. See `work/data/index_daily/sector_etf_nav_price_tri_panel_2023_2026.csv` and `outputs/sector_etf_nav_price_tri_comparison.csv`.

From 3 Apr 2023 through 23 Oct 2026, NAV close-to-close returns ranged from +19.59% (ITBEES) to +113.29% (PHARMABEES), against +20.60% to +121.13% for the corresponding gross sector TRI. In the 2025+ holdout window, NAV and TRI returns differed by -0.31 to -1.26 percentage points. These are unlevered, no-fee comparisons on reported NAV dates, not a reinvested ETF total-return reconstruction. NAV can reflect fund expenses and valuation, but it does not replace a verified cash-distribution ledger or model market-price fills. The existing sector strategy results remain unchanged and price-only.

## Fees and execution implications

As displayed on 27 Oct 2026, Upstox lists equity intraday brokerage at ₹20 per executed order or 0.1% (whichever is lower), NSE equity transaction charges at 0.00307% from 1 Mar 2026, sell-side equity-intraday STT at 0.025%, buy-side stamp duty at 0.003%, SEBI turnover fees at ₹10/crore, and GST at 18% on applicable charges ([Upstox brokerage charges](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/)). NSE's delivery STT table distinguishes the instrument: equity shares pay 0.1% on both sides, while equity-oriented-fund units pay nil on purchase and 0.001% on sale; AMFI says other scheme units such as Gold ETFs are not subject to STT. DP charges apply on delivery sells. Recalculate both legs and other applicable components per order. The page documents dated rate changes, so applying the current schedule to all prior years is a present-cost stress, not a historical contract-note reconstruction. See the [fee-model audit](../work/research/fee_model_audit.md).

## Gates for a defensible sector-rotation replay

1. Use official sector TRI for total-return benchmarking; keep fills on ETF market prices. The saved AMFI NAV history supplies a fund-valuation cross-check only from Apr 2023 and has five unmatched market dates per fund.
2. Use the common live ETF universe only from its first actual bar (currently 25 Jan 2022 for all five listed funds); do not backfill a new ETF using an index without labeling the result as an index proxy.
3. Resolve the disputed AUTOBEES/PHARMABEES event claims with an official AMC, AMFI, or exchange record before claiming complete ETF total returns; AMFI's current selected event requests returned empty lists, which do not guarantee completeness.
4. Use ISIN/instrument-key joins across the ITBEES symbol rename and record API coverage gaps; Upstox’s API documentation does not guarantee adjustment or event-history completeness.
5. Keep fee assumptions dated or explicitly label uniform current fees as a stress; fixed slippage is not observed spread.
