## Data integrity correction — 2026-10-27

Upstox daily ETF bars sometimes carried within-session timestamps, so the previous multi-ETF exact-timestamp join created extra rows for the same trading date. The quarterly NIFTY/Gold control had 3,232 rows for 3,166 unique NIFTY sessions. The daily loader now keys `-days-1-` files by local session date, deduplicates only identical same-day OHLCV rows, and stops on conflicting daily records. The official [Upstox Historical Candle V3 documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/) defines the timestamp as the candle-period start and illustrates daily candles at 00:00 IST.

The date-aligned reruns now also include 90 India VIX overlay cases. NSE's official daily history resolves the three conflicting Upstox sessions; the original cache is preserved, and the overlay uses a separate reconciled input. At 50 bps per side, every full-history VIX variant breaches the 20% close-drawdown limit. At 10 bps, the no-overlay baseline has the highest return with the same drawdown as every overlay, so VIX is not added to the candidate rule. The detailed integrity review and corrected risk screen are in [the timestamp and OHLC audit](etf_daily_timestamp_and_intraday_risk_audit.md).

## ETF STT correction — 2026-10-27

The first shared ETF fee model treated every ETF unit like an equity share. NSE’s current schedule charges equity shares 0.1% STT on both delivery sides, equity-oriented-fund units nil on purchase and 0.001% on sale, and 0.025% on an intraday sale of an equity-oriented-fund unit. AMFI says STT does not apply to other mutual-fund schemes; Gold ETFs are listed as non-equity funds in its tax guidance. The corrected model classifies NIFTYBEES and the equity-sector ETFs as equity-fund units and GOLDBEES/LIQUIDBEES as other fund units. Integer share quantities and every dependent replay were recalculated. See the [fee-model audit](../work/research/fee_model_audit.md), [NSE levy schedule](https://www.nseindia.com/static/invest/first-time-investor-sebi-turnover-fees-stt-other-levies), and [AMFI tax guidance](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=TaxRegimeForMutualFunds).

The direct Upstox research runner reads `UPSTOX_ANALYTICS_TOKEN` from the HKCU registry when it is absent from the process environment. The token is never written to research artifacts. Historical backtest inputs remain frozen through 2026-10-23. A 2026-10-30 refresh returned four candles per ETF through Oct 29, producing two post-freeze common sessions and 30 frozen scenario rows. A separate exact-date request for Oct 30 returned no candles for either ETF; this does not establish a holiday or feed completeness, and the paper runner still excludes the current date. This short paper window is not performance evidence; no live broker orders were placed. Request metadata is redacted and saved locally.

## Executive readout

No strategy is established as a reliable winner. In the extended 2014–2026 benchmark, the passive initial 22.5% NIFTYBEES / 22.5% GOLDBEES mix returned +145.62% after modeled costs and passed the historical OHLC screen, but its exposure drifted from 45% to about 79.45% peak and 76.2% at the end. The quarterly-rebalanced static 50/50 mix at a 45% exposure cap returned +103.18% with a -12.91% envelope. Among fixed NIFTY/Gold trend rules, the 74% gross cap returned +111.65% but left only 0.009 percentage points of historical headroom under 20%; the 70% cap returned +107.75% and left 0.93 points, while the 45% cap returned +77.77%, left 3.85 points, and had the lowest bootstrap breach frequency among these fixed caps. These are retrospective price-based results with modeled 50 bps per-side slippage; the risk screen is not a future loss limit.

An expanding-window annual selector at a fixed 45% cap grew ₹10 lakh to ₹15.49 lakh (+54.89%) over the 2020–2026 test sequence, versus ₹16.26 lakh (+62.56%) for fixed D55/20. The full-sequence conservative OHLC bound was -14.86% for both, but selection did not improve the control. See the [walk-forward method and annual results](nifty_gold_walk_forward_method.md).

The 100% fixed quarterly control returned +137.64%, but its close drawdown was -17.86% and its conservative OHLC envelope was -25.66%. The 100% inverse-vol version returned +139.85%, with -18.11% close drawdown and -25.55% OHLC bound. Two full-history channel variants returned more but also breached the OHLC screen. Higher return does not qualify if the stated 20% risk constraint fails.
