## Data integrity correction — 2026-10-27

Upstox daily ETF bars sometimes carried within-session timestamps, so the previous multi-ETF exact-timestamp join created extra rows for the same trading date. The quarterly NIFTY/Gold control had 3,232 rows for 3,166 unique NIFTY sessions. The official [Upstox Historical Candle V3 documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/) defines the timestamp as the candle-period start and illustrates daily candles at 00:00 IST.

## ETF STT correction — 2026-10-27

The first shared ETF fee model treated every ETF unit like an equity share. NSE’s current schedule charges equity shares 0.1% STT on both delivery sides, equity-oriented-fund units nil on purchase and 0.001% on sale, and 0.025% on an intraday sale of an equity-oriented-fund unit. AMFI says STT does not apply to other mutual-fund schemes; Gold ETFs are listed as non-equity funds in its tax guidance. The corrected model classifies NIFTYBEES and the equity-sector ETFs as equity-fund units and GOLDBEES/LIQUIDBEES as other fund units. Integer share quantities and every dependent replay were recalculated. See the [fee-model audit](../work/research/fee_model_audit.md), [NSE levy schedule](https://www.nseindia.com/static/invest/first-time-investor-sebi-turnover-fees-stt-other-levies), and [AMFI tax guidance](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=TaxRegimeForMutualFunds).

The direct Upstox research runner reads `UPSTOX_ANALYTICS_TOKEN` from the HKCU registry when it is absent from the process environment. The token is never written to research artifacts. Historical backtest inputs remain frozen through 2026-10-23. A 2026-10-30 refresh returned four candles per ETF through Oct 29, producing two post-freeze common sessions and 30 frozen scenario rows. A separate exact-date request for Oct 30 returned no candles for either ETF; this does not establish a holiday or feed completeness, and the paper runner still excludes the current date. This short paper window is not performance evidence; no live broker orders were placed. Request metadata is redacted and saved locally.

## Method and costs

- Most daily ETF strategies use close-based signals, next-session-open execution, whole-unit sizing, cash-account exposure caps, and terminal liquidation. Slippage varies by screen; the current NIFTY/Gold risk table uses 50 bps per side. ETF tests do not short.
- **Donchian 55/20:** enter when close exceeds the prior 55-session high; exit when close falls below the prior 20-session low. **200DMA trend:** invested when close is above its 200-session average. **12–1 momentum:** at month end, rank 12-month return excluding the latest month, keep positive-return ETFs above their 200DMA, and equal-weight the top three.
- Walk-forward exposure choices were restricted to 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, or 95%. The selected cap maximized validation CAGR among choices whose train, validation, and continuous train-plus-validation drawdowns each stayed within 20%.
- Splits: (A) train 2014–2017, validate 2018–2019, test 2020–2026; (B) train 2014–2019, validate 2020–2022, test 2023–2026. Since both test windows were inspected while comparing strategies, they are **retrospective tests**, not untouched holdouts now.
- The NIFTY options rule uses the index 200DMA signal 20 trading sessions before monthly expiry, buys one 200-point ATM debit spread at the next session open, and closes five sessions before expiry. Marks use daily closes; 50 bps per leg per side is the base with 25/100 bps sensitivity. Two expiry periods were skipped: Dec 2024 had empty expired-contract candles; Apr 2025 had no monthly chain. Both debit- and credit-spread fees use fill-date rates from the shared helper.
- **Reproducibility update:** the earlier exploratory options summary showed -2.52% full sample / -0.59% holdout. Its complete signal-history file had not been retained. After saving NIFTY 50 daily history from 2014–2026 and applying date-aware STT and transaction/IPFT rates, the debit-spread replay returned -3.39% / -0.77% at 50 bps. The 19-trade credit vertical returned -0.74% at 50 bps. Both remain negative net of fees and slippage; the current replays supersede older figures.
- ETF backtests apply the current Upstox/NSE schedule uniformly over historical trades as a cost stress, with instrument-specific STT for equity shares, equity-oriented fund units, and non-equity fund units. The separate futures calculation applies published fee dates; monthly option replays now date-gate STT and NSE transaction/IPFT fees from 2024-10 onward.
