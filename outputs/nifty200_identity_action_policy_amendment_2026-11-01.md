## Identity and price-unit policy

Load predecessor/successor raw and adjusted histories before filtering to the identities active in a membership interval. The point-in-time history table determines the exchange symbol and ISIN for each date; a current or first-ever symbol is never applied to the full past. A symbol rename on the same ISIN preserves the exchange identity. A split/bonus bridge changes the share count and raw-price unit by its evidenced ratio on the economic effective date, while preserving the account value at the conversion. A merger, demerger, rights issue, or other non-equivalent entitlement remains a separate path until its cash/share proceeds are evidenced. Do not union unrelated issuers or fetch a price for a retired ISIN.

For signal history, convert predecessor prices into the signal-date share unit using only verified split/bonus ratios. For an open position, convert quantity at the official economic effective date, then move any pending rebalance to the date-valid ISIN and raw open. Preserve both the economic date and the first observed new-ISIN date. If two identities are simultaneously present in the same family/date, an order or position lacks a unique evidenced identifier, or a required price remains absent, stop that path before the affected trade/mark.

## Corporate-action acceptance statuses

Each exact action record receives one of three statuses: **supported under documented source assumptions**, **requires exceptional review**, or **demonstrably invalid**. The first status means the vendor event record and stated price-adjustment model are internally coherent; it does not claim that the feed is ground truth or complete.

The factor comparison tolerance is fixed before outcomes at `numpy.isclose(expected, observed, rtol=2e-4, atol=2e-5)`. For a split, the expected adjacent factor multiplier is `old_face_value / new_face_value`; the raw-price continuity check is reported as a realized ex-date return after that unit conversion, not forced to zero. A mismatch beyond tolerance remains an exception.

- Deduplicate only exact duplicate records by date-valid issuer/symbol, vendor ISIN, ex-date, type, and every normalized term field (ratio numerator/denominator, cash amount, and face-value terms). Distinct dividend amounts on one ex-date are retained and summed after exact duplicates are removed. Multiple conflicting split/bonus terms remain separate exceptions.
- Routine dividends with finite non-negative terms, a valid prior close, and aggregate cash below that close are supported under the documented cash-adjustment convention: sum same-date payouts first, then use `previous_close / (previous_close - aggregate_cash)`. The related provider adjusted-factor change must match within the registered tolerance in the run manifest. Cash credited to a held account is separately computed from the shares entitled on the ex-date; it is not double-counted when the signal already uses adjusted prices.
- Splits and bonuses require finite positive face-value/ratio terms, an evidenced share conversion, and adjusted-factor/raw-price unit checks. The factor convention is applied once per event; same-date cash payouts do not multiply separate dividend factors.
- Rights, mergers, demergers/spinoffs, unknown `other` actions, contradictory terms, uncertain identity, missing terms, unusually large dividends (cash exceeding 10% of the prior close), and factor/price-unit mismatches enter the exception queue. They are reconciled if they touch a ranking-changing signal, intended order, held position, or valuation. AGM-only and buyback notices do not create a price/share factor unless the notice also contains an independently parsed distribution/entitlement.
- Resolve each action against the point-in-time symbol-history row for its ISIN and ex-date. If the vendor symbol is stale but the ISIN has one unique date-valid issuer/symbol, retain the vendor label and use that dated identity. If the action ISIN is stale, use only an exact reviewed action-to-identity bridge; never attach an event to the first or nearest ISIN. Record the provider's first new-ISIN observation separately from the economic ex-date and the official effective date.
- A material missing score is never assigned zero or silently omitted. A candidate can be treated as ineligible only when the frozen rule has positive, finite, date-valid observed records throughout its explicit lookback. Any unknown event or identity that could change selection stops that configuration at the signal date unless a strict ranking bound proves it cannot affect the frozen target.

## Protocol v1.1 pre-outcome action-identity clarification

This clarification was recorded before any strategy return or P&L outcome was calculated. The four signal rules, universes, schedules, holdings, costs, and evaluation period remain unchanged.

When a vendor action ISIN has no unique active history row on its ex-date, an exact vendor event symbol may identify the action only if that symbol maps to exactly one date-valid active ISIN in the point-in-time history on that ex-date. The vendor ISIN and symbol are retained unchanged as reported, and the identity discrepancy is recorded. No nearest-ISIN, first-ever-symbol, or future alias lookup is permitted. Routine dividend, split, and bonus acceptance additionally requires finite event terms, the active date-valid identity's exact adjacent raw/adjusted records, and the pre-registered factor formula and tolerance to match. The raw ex-date return after share-unit conversion is retained as observed; it is not forced to zero. This is a documented source/model assumption, not issuer-level proof or ground truth.

For a reviewed ISIN change, the bridge ledger keeps the existing `effective_date` as the point-in-time vendor-history successor boundary used by the replay; it separately records `official_trade_isin_effective_date` when the exchange notice states when the new identifier applies to trades, the economic ex-date, the record date, and the vendor's first successor-row observation. The three 2017 MSE circulars state that the new ISIN applies from the earlier ex-date even though the history table first observes it on the following session. These dates are not substituted for one another.

This exact-symbol path can support routine dividend/split/bonus signal inputs after formula validation. It does not resolve rights, mergers, demergers, spinoffs, missing or contradictory terms, ambiguous active identities, or a factor/term mismatch; those remain exceptional-review dependencies until source evidence closes the affected path. No such exception is classified as demonstrably invalid without independent contradictory evidence.
